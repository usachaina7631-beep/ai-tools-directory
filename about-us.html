What this site is

AI Tools Directory (aitoolsduniya.in) is an independent, curated directory of AI tools. It helps students, creators and small teams in India and everywhere else find the right AI tool for a task — writing, video, images, audio, coding, study and business — without opening fifty tabs first.

The directory currently lists 84 tools across 10 categories, most of them free or with a free plan. Every listing has a short description written by us in our own words and a link to the tool's official website.

How tools are chosen and listed

  • Real tools, official links: a tool is listed only when it has a working official website. Links always go to the official site, never to a download copy or a look-alike page.
  • Original descriptions: every description on this site is written by us. We do not copy text from the tool's marketing pages or from other directories.
  • Honest free labels: tools are labelled Free, Free Plan or Paid based on what the official site offers. Pricing can change, so we ask readers to always confirm the current price on the official site before paying.
  • Regular clean-up: listings are re-checked and corrected when a tool changes its name, website or pricing, and stale entries are fixed or removed.

Who runs this site

This directory is built and maintained by Rajan Kumar, an independent creator from Bihar, India. The same person runs the listings, the corrections and the guides published on this site. There is no big team and no paid ranking: a tool's position in the directory is not sold.

Sponsored placements are always labelled “Advertisement”. Affiliate relationships never decide whether a tool is listed or what its description says.
