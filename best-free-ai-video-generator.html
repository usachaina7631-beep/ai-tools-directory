Best Free AI Video Generators in 2026 — What "Free" Really Gives You

By Rajan Kumar, AI Tools Directory · Last checked: 9 November 2026 · Prices and free plans can change at any time.

Quick Answer

If you want one free AI video generator to start with today, start with PixVerse — it is one of the few tools that still gives you fresh credits every day (60 credits at sign-up, then 30 credits a day). For the most realistic motion, try Kling AI on its free plan. Save your Runway free credits for one special clip, because they are given only once and never refill. And if your real work is editing videos for YouTube Shorts or Reels, CapCut is free in the way that actually matters — it is a full editor, not a credit-burning generator.

One warning before the list: two famous names, Pika and Hailuo, no longer give any monthly free credits at all, and Sora has been discontinued. Many older articles on the internet still call them free. They are out of date.

How We Checked

Every free limit, watermark rule and price in this article was checked in November 2026 against the tool's own pricing page, help centre or terms — not copied from other blogs. The Tools in Detail

1. PixVerse — the best free plan for daily practice

PixVerse gives 60 credits when you sign up and 30 fresh credits every day. They do not roll over, so the habit that works is simple: use them daily or lose them. The output is up to 540p on the free plan, downloads carry a watermark, and free videos are for personal use only — commercial use needs a paid plan (Standard is $10/month, or $8/month billed yearly). For learning prompts and posting practice clips to Shorts and Reels, this is currently the most usable free deal.

2. Kling AI — the most realistic free motion

Kling AI, from Kuaishou, is known for smooth, realistic motion and strong image-to-video. Its free Basic plan lets logged-in users claim free credits (the exact batch changes and is valid for a limited time), output is capped at 720p, videos are watermarked, and commercial use is for paid members only. One correction to what you may have read elsewhere: the famous "66 free credits a day" figure is outdated — do not plan around it. The Standard plan is about $8.80/month and is one of the cheaper serious upgrades.

3. Runway — save it for one special clip

Runway's Gen-4 models are what many filmmakers reach for, and its free plan gives 125 credits. The catch is big: those credits are one-time, they never refresh, and they cover roughly 25 seconds of Gen-4 Turbo video in total. Free videos carry a watermark and export at 720p. Unusually, Runway's terms do allow commercial use even on the free plan — but the watermark stays. Our advice: practise on PixVerse or Kling first, then spend your Runway credits once, on the one clip that matters. Standard costs $15/month ($12/month billed yearly).

4. Luma Dream Machine — realistic scenes, changing plans

Luma's Ray models make photorealistic text-to-video and image-to-video, and the free tier gives limited credits at draft quality in a slower queue. Free videos are watermarked — permanently; upgrading later does not remove the watermark from a clip you already made — and free use is personal only. Luma has been changing its plan structure recently, so check its pricing page for the current entry price before you decide.

5. Canva (Magic Media) — five clips, for designs

Canva's Magic Media can turn text into a video clip, but on the free plan you get only 5 video generations in total — not per month, in total. Each clip is 4 seconds and silent. The good news: the clip itself has no watermark, and Canva's AI terms allow you to use the output for any lawful purpose, as long as you use it inside your own design and do not sell the element on its own. Best use: a tiny moving visual inside a presentation, thumbnail background or social post — not a video channel.

6. CapCut — the free editor that beats generators for real work

CapCut is not mainly a text-to-video generator; it is a free editor (phone, desktop and web) with AI captions, text-to-speech and some AI generation tools on top. A standard export of your own footage has no watermark, which is why most Shorts and Reels creators can do their entire job here for ₹0. Two cautions: Pro-marked effects and templates can add a watermark or block export on the free plan, and the music matters legally — if a project uses a track marked for non-commercial use, the whole video becomes non-commercial. Check the label on sounds before you post a monetised video.

7. InVideo AI — one prompt, one full faceless video

InVideo AI turns a single prompt into a complete video — script, stock footage, voiceover and editing in one flow. The free plan allows roughly 10 minutes of AI generation per week, at 720p, and every free export carries a hard watermark that cannot be removed. Commercial use needs a paid plan (Plus, $25/month or about $20/month billed yearly, which also removes the watermark and gives 1080p). It is one of the fastest routes for faceless YouTube videos — once you are ready to pay. On free, use it to test whether the format works for you.

8. Fliki — voiceover videos, a very small free plan

Fliki specialises in text-to-video with AI voices and avatars, popular for faceless channels. Be clear about the free plan before you start: it is only 3 credits a month (about 5 minutes of audio/video by Fliki's own FAQ), at 720p, with a watermark, and no commercial rights. The Standard plan ($28/month, $21/month billed yearly) is where Fliki actually becomes usable, with 1080p and a commercial licence. We list Fliki in our directory because the paid product is genuinely good for Hindi and English voiceover videos — but its free plan is a demo, not a tool. Free in name only: Pika and Hailuo

Two tools appear in almost every "best free AI video generator" list on the internet, and in November 2026 neither one gives you monthly free generation. Pika's free account currently includes 0 credits per month — you must buy a credit pack or subscribe before generating. Hailuo (MiniMax) also gives 0 monthly credits; new users get a one-time welcome pack that expires just 3 days after it is granted. Both are good tools on paid plans (Pika's Starter is $10/month; Hailuo's Standard is around $10.50–14.99/month) — just do not build a free workflow on them based on an old blog post.

What "Free" Actually Costs You

Free AI video almost always has three hidden costs. First, watermarks are the default — of our ten tools, only Canva's own clips and CapCut's standard exports avoid them. Second, "free credits" mean three different things: daily (PixVerse), weekly (InVideo AI) or one-time, gone forever (Runway, Canva). Third, commercial rights almost never come free — only Runway and Canva allow commercial use on their free plans, with conditions. Add one more real-world cost: AI video has a discard rate. Even a good creator throws away several generations for every clip that is worth posting, so your real free allowance is smaller than the credit number suggests.

For Indian Creators — Hindi, Payment and Mobile

This is the section most global guides skip. A few practical notes from our own daily work making AI videos in India:

  • Hindi prompts work, Hindi planning works better. Most generators accept Hindi or Hinglish prompts, but the most reliable results still come from writing the scene description in simple English and keeping your on-screen hook text and voiceover in Hindi. Test the tool's voice list for Hindi before paying for a voiceover tool — not every "300 voices" library has a good Hindi voice.
  • Payment is the real gate, not the tool. Most of these services charge in US dollars and expect an international card; UPI is rarely accepted directly. A tool with a slightly worse free plan but an easy payment route can be the practical winner when you upgrade.
  • Your phone is enough to start. CapCut's app plus PixVerse or Kling in the mobile browser covers a complete Shorts workflow. You do not need a PC or a graphics card to begin — the open-source route (self-hosted models) is the only path here that demands a strong GPU, which is why we do not recommend it for beginners.

5 Prompt Tips That Save Your Free Credits

  1. Use cinematic language — describe the shot like a camera operator: "slow dolly-in, golden hour light," not just "a nice video."
  2. Specify the motion — say exactly what moves and how: who walks, what turns, what the camera does.
  3. Define the place and mood — location, time of day, weather and atmosphere in the prompt, every time.
  4. Keep one subject consistent — describe your character the same way in every prompt if the clip is part of a series.
  5. Practise on the daily-credit tool, spend on the premium one — refine your prompt on PixVerse's daily credits, then use the final version where credits are one-time.

Free vs Paid — When Should You Upgrade?

Stay free while you are learning and testing formats. Upgrade when any one of these becomes true: you are posting on a schedule and daily credits run out; you need to remove the watermark for a monetised channel; or you need commercial rights for client work — remember, on most tools the commercial licence starts only with a paid plan. When you do upgrade, the cheapest sensible steps in this list are Kling Standard (about $8.80/month), PixVerse Standard ($10/month) and Pika Starter ($10/month). Do not buy a yearly plan in your first month with any tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free AI video generator?

For most beginners, PixVerse, because its credits refresh daily. For realism, Kling AI. For one premium clip, Runway — but its free credits never refill.

Is there a free AI video generator with no watermark?

Almost none for generated clips. Canva's Magic Media clips have no watermark (but you get only 5, ever), and CapCut exports of your own footage are watermark-free. Everything else watermarks free output.

Is there a completely free, unlimited AI video generator?

No hosted tool offers that in 2026. The only truly unlimited route is running an open-source model on your own powerful computer, which needs a good GPU and technical setup.

Can I use free AI-generated videos commercially?

Usually, no. Of the tools compared here, only Runway and Canva allow commercial use on free plans, with conditions. On all others, commercial rights start with a paid plan.

How long a video can I make for free?

Generated clips are short — typically 4 to 10 seconds per generation. Full-length videos are made by generating clips and joining them in an editor like CapCut, or by using a prompt-to-video tool such as InVideo AI within its weekly free minutes.

Is Google Veo free to use?

Google keeps moving its free video access between products — free generation in the Gemini app has closed, and access has shifted towards Google Flow, with limits that change often. Check Flow's own page for the current free allowance rather than trusting any guide.

Is Sora free? What about Grok Imagine?

No. Sora has been discontinued, and Grok Imagine's free access ended in March 2026. Any article still recommending them as free options is out of date.

How much does AI video cost once I pay?

Entry plans cluster around $8–15/month for the generators in this list. What matters more than the headline price is the credit count and whether the watermark and commercial licence are included — check those two lines before the price.

Conclusion

"Free" in AI video no longer means what it did a year ago. The honest picture in November 2026: PixVerse and Kling give you a real free starting point, Runway and Canva give you a small but valuable free taste, CapCut quietly does the most free work of all, and two famous names — Pika and Hailuo — are free in name only. Start with the tool that matches your use case, learn prompting on daily credits, and pay only when the watermark or the commercial licence is actually blocking your growth.
