Audio & Voice tools

ElevenLabs Freemium

Natural text-to-speech and voice cloning used for narration, audiobooks and video voiceovers.

Best for: The most natural-sounding AI voices — for narration, audiobooks and video voiceovers.

Tags: Text-to-Speech, Voice Clone · Official website: https://elevenlabs.io

Suno Freemium

Generate complete songs — lyrics, vocals and music — from a simple text prompt.

Best for: Creating a complete song — lyrics, vocals and music — from one line of text.

Tags: Music, Songs · Official website: https://suno.com

Udio Freemium

Music generation tool for creating original tracks in many genres from text descriptions.

Tags: Music, Tracks · Official website: https://www.udio.com

Whisper Free

OpenAI's open-source speech recognition — transcribe audio to text in many languages.

Tags: Transcription, Open Source · Official website: https://openai.com/research/whisper

Murf AI Freemium

Studio-quality voiceovers for explainer videos, e-learning and presentations.

Tags: Voiceover, Narration · Official website: https://murf.ai

Speechify Freemium

Turns text into natural-sounding audio — Speechify reads articles, PDFs and books aloud in realistic AI voices, so you can listen while you walk or study.

Tags: Text-to-Speech, Audiobooks, Listening · Official website: https://speechify.com

Frequently Asked Questions

Which audio tool here is completely free?

Whisper — OpenAI's open-source speech recognition for transcribing audio to text in many languages. The other five audio tools are Freemium: they have free plans or trials.

Which tool has the most natural AI voices?

ElevenLabs. It is listed as best for the most natural-sounding AI voices — for narration, audiobooks and video voiceovers.

Which tool makes full songs?

Suno generates complete songs — lyrics, vocals and music — from a simple text prompt. Udio also creates original tracks in many genres from text descriptions.

Which tool reads articles aloud?

Speechify. It turns text into natural-sounding audio and reads articles, PDFs and books aloud in realistic AI voices, so you can listen while you walk or study.
