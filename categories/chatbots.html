ChatGPT Freemium

Conversational assistant for answers, writing, brainstorming, coding help and everyday problem-solving.

Best for: Everyday questions, first drafts and brainstorming — the best all-rounder to start any task with.

Tags: Chat, Writing, Coding · Official website: https://chatgpt.com

Claude Freemium

Thoughtful AI assistant known for long-document analysis, careful writing and nuanced reasoning.

Best for: Reading long PDFs and reports and turning them into clear summaries and careful writing — great for students and writers.

Tags: Chat, Documents, Reasoning · Official website: https://claude.ai

Google Gemini Freemium

Google's multimodal assistant for search-style answers, drafting, image understanding and Workspace help.

Best for: Fresh, search-style answers, understanding photos, and help inside Gmail and Docs.

Tags: Chat, Multimodal, Search · Official website: https://gemini.google.com

Microsoft Copilot Freemium

AI companion built into Windows and Microsoft 365 for summaries, drafts and quick answers.

Tags: Chat, Office, Windows · Official website: https://copilot.microsoft.com

Poe Freemium

One app to chat with many leading AI models and community-built bots side by side.

Tags: Multi-model, Chat, Bots · Official website: https://poe.com

Ask AI Free

Free Hindi-English AI assistant by an Indian creator — chat, translation, YouTube scripts and SEO, business help, study help and research on your own text, on the web and Telegram, with voice input.

Best for: Free questions, answers and study help on Telegram — no app install needed.

Tags: Hindi, Chatbot, Telegram, Voice Input · Official website: https://vishal-ai-assistant.onrender.com

DeepSeek Free

Free AI chatbot with strong reasoning for writing, coding, maths and study help — open-weight models, web chat and mobile app.

Best for: Strong reasoning and coding answers that are completely free — the best zero-cost starting point.

Tags: AI Chat, Reasoning, Coding · Official website: https://deepseek.com

Grok Freemium

xAI's AI chatbot — quick answers, real-time information from X, image generation and voice mode, with a free tier on grok.com and in the X app.

Tags: AI Chat, Real-time, Image Generation · Official website: https://grok.com

Frequently Asked Questions

Which chatbot tools in this list are completely free?

Ask AI — a free Hindi-English assistant — and DeepSeek, a free chatbot with strong reasoning. The other six chatbots are Freemium: they have a free plan or trial, and paid plans unlock more usage.

ChatGPT or Claude — which should I pick?

ChatGPT is the all-rounder in this list: everyday questions, first drafts and brainstorming. Claude is the pick for long documents — it is listed as best for reading long PDFs and reports and turning them into clear summaries. Both are Freemium, so the practical way to choose is to try the same task on both free plans.

Which chatbot is best for study help?

DeepSeek is free and listed as strong for reasoning, writing, coding, maths and study help. Ask AI is free and lists study help, translation and YouTube scripts. Claude is listed as best for turning long PDFs and reports into summaries — useful for notes and textbooks.

Do these chatbots work in Hindi?

Ask AI is the Hindi-English assistant in this list — it is built for Hindi and English chat, translation and study help. For the other chatbots, this directory does not list Hindi support, so check the tool's official website before deciding.
