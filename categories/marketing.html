AdCreative.ai Paid

Generates ad banners and copy designed to convert, for social and display campaigns.

Tags: Ads, Banners · Official website: https://www.adcreative.ai

Surfer SEO Paid

Content editor that scores articles against top-ranking pages to improve search rankings.

Tags: SEO, Content · Official website: https://surfer.ai

Simplified Freemium

All-in-one AI suite for writing, design, video and social-media scheduling, built for creators and small teams.

Best for: Writing, design and video in one place — the all-in-one pick for small teams.

Tags: AI Writer, Design, Video · Official website: https://simplified.com (affiliate link — we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you)

OpusClip Freemium

Cuts long videos into viral short clips with captions, scored for social platforms.

Best for: Automatically cutting viral Shorts out of long videos — a big time-saver for podcasters and streamers.

Tags: Shorts, Clipping · Official website: https://www.opus.pro

Google Ads Account Summary Free

Google's free reporting solution — builds an at-a-glance summary of your whole Google Ads account and can email a daily HTML performance report.

Tags: Google Ads, Reports, Email · Official website: https://developers.google.com/google-ads/scripts/docs/solutions/account-summary

Google Ads Ad Performance Free

Google's free tool that builds a Google Sheet with distribution charts of ad performance — handy for comparing which headlines and final URLs work best.

Tags: Google Ads, Reports, Charts · Official website: https://developers.google.com/google-ads/scripts/docs/solutions/ad-performance

Google Ads Anomaly Detector Free

Google's free alert tool for Ads accounts — compares today's clicks, cost, impressions and conversions with the same weekday's past average and emails you when something drops or spikes unusually.

Tags: Google Ads, Alerts, Automation · Official website: https://developers.google.com/google-ads/scripts/docs/solutions/account-anomaly-detector

Google Ads Link Checker Free

Google's free checker that tests URLs in your ads, keywords and sitelinks and alerts you when a link returns a page-not-found or other error — so paid clicks never land on a broken page.

Tags: Google Ads, Link Check, Alerts · Official website: https://developers.google.com/google-ads/scripts/docs/solutions/link-checker

Google Ads Flexible Budgets Free

Google's free budget solution — spreads a campaign's total budget across a date range with your own daily distribution scheme, instead of one flat daily budget.

Tags: Google Ads, Budgets, Automation · Official website: https://developers.google.com/google-ads/scripts/docs/solutions/flexible-budgets

Google Ads Common Negative List Free

Google's free tool to manage one shared negative keyword and placement list from a spreadsheet and apply it across many campaigns at once.

Tags: Google Ads, Negative Keywords, Automation · Official website: https://developers.google.com/google-ads/scripts/docs/solutions/common-negative-list
