Research tools

Perplexity Freemium

Answer engine that replies with cited sources — ideal for quick research and fact-checking.

Best for: Fact-checked research with cited sources — when you need proof along with the answer.

Tags: Search, Citations, Research · Official website: https://www.perplexity.ai

Elicit Freemium

Research assistant that finds academic papers and extracts key findings into tables.

Tags: Papers, Literature Review · Official website: https://elicit.com

Consensus Freemium

Search engine over peer-reviewed studies that answers questions with evidence summaries.

Tags: Studies, Evidence · Official website: https://consensus.app

ChatPDF Freemium

Upload a PDF and chat with it — ask questions and get answers from the document itself.

Best for: Asking questions directly to one PDF — get the point without reading the whole file.

Tags: PDF, Q&A · Official website: https://www.chatpdf.com

NotebookLM Freemium

Google's AI research notebook — upload your notes, PDFs and links, then get summaries, answers grounded in your own sources, Audio Overviews and mind maps.

Best for: Studying from your own uploaded notes and books — the most useful study companion for exam preparation.

Tags: Notes, Research, Summaries · Official website: https://notebooklm.google.com
