Frequently Asked Questions

Which video tools here are free?

None of the 18 video tools is listed as fully free. All 18 have free plans or trials — CapCut, Kling AI, Pika, Luma Dream Machine and the rest all start free, with paid plans for more usage.

Which video tool is best for Shorts and Reels?

CapCut is listed as best for everyday Shorts and Reels editing — auto captions, effects and background removal in one free app. Submagic is the other pick for Shorts: big, stylish captions that make viewers stop scrolling.

Which video tool makes the most realistic clips?

Kling AI is listed as best for realistic, cinematic short clips — lifelike motion is its signature. Veo 3.1 is the other realistic pick: high-quality AI video clips generated with sound.

Can I make a video without appearing on camera?

Yes. HeyGen makes talking AI presenter videos from a script, Fliki turns a script into a voiced video for faceless channels, Pictory turns a blog post or script into a stock-footage video, and InVideo turns a script or idea into a ready video with stock footage and voiceover.
