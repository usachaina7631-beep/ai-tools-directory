Writing tools

Grammarly Freemium

Writing assistant that fixes grammar, tone and clarity across email, docs and social posts.

Best for: Fixing English writing mistakes — the pre-send check for emails, assignments and resumes.

Tags: Grammar, Editing, Tone · Official website: https://www.grammarly.com

QuillBot Freemium

Paraphrasing and summarizing tool that helps rewrite sentences in different tones.

Best for: Rewriting text in fresh words and making quick summaries — a student favourite.

Tags: Paraphrase, Summarize · Official website: https://quillbot.com

Jasper Paid

Marketing-focused AI writer for ad copy, blog posts and brand-consistent campaigns.

Tags: Marketing Copy, Blogs · Official website: https://www.jasper.ai

Copy.ai Freemium

Go-to-market AI writer for sales emails, social captions and product descriptions.

Tags: Sales Copy, Social · Official website: https://www.copy.ai

Notion AI Paid

AI inside Notion that drafts, summarizes and organizes notes, docs and wikis.

Tags: Notes, Docs, Summary · Official website: https://www.notion.com/product/ai

Rytr Freemium

AI writing assistant that drafts blog posts, emails, ad copy and social captions in 30+ languages from a short brief — built for creators and small teams who need first drafts fast.

Tags: Blog Writing, Emails, Ad Copy · Official website: https://rytr.me

DeepL Freemium

AI translator that renders text and whole documents in 30+ languages with natural, context-aware wording — free translator with a Pro plan for unlimited document translation.

Tags: Translation, AI Translator, Multilingual · Official website: https://www.deepl.com

Writesonic Freemium

AI writing platform that drafts SEO articles, ads and product descriptions from a short brief, with a free plan to start.

Tags: SEO Writing, Blog Posts, Ad Copy · Official website: https://writesonic.com

Which tool translates documents?

QuillBot. It is listed as best for rewriting text in fresh words and making quick summaries — a student favourite.
