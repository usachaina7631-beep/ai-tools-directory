Privacy Policy

AI Tools Directory — aitoolsduniya.in · Last updated: 16 November 2026

What this site is

AI Tools Directory (aitoolsduniya.in) is an independent, curated directory website. It lists AI tools with short original descriptions and links to each tool's official website, for discovery purposes only. Tool names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. This same policy applies to our Android app, which simply opens this website.

Information we collect

We do not run a server that stores your personal data, and we do not sell personal information.

  • No real accounts: the sign-in / sign-up shown on this site is a demo. As the site itself states, your account stays in your own browser tab only and no password is sent anywhere.
  • Favourites, ratings and similar on-page choices: these are kept in your own browser on your own device. They are not sent to us or stored on a server by us.
  • Admin dashboard changes: changes made in the on-page admin view apply only to your own browser session's view.
  • Email you send us: if you contact us or submit a tool through the email links on this site, your email app opens and you choose what to send. We receive only the details you yourself write in that email.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand which pages and tools are popular, in aggregate. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies and collects usage information such as pages visited, approximate region, device and browser type, and clicks on outbound tool links. This helps us improve the directory. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Advertising and cookies

This site is set up to display advertising through Google AdSense. When ads are shown, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com), and you can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at google.com/policies/technologies/partner-sites/.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links — for example, some tool links carry a referral code. If you buy or subscribe through one of them, this directory may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Sponsored placements on this site are labelled “Advertisement”. An affiliate link does not change what data this site itself collects about you; any purchase happens on the tool's own website.

External websites

Most links on this site take you to third-party tool websites. Those websites have their own privacy policies and data practices, which we do not control. When you sign up, pay or share information on a tool's website, that tool's own privacy policy applies — please read it there before paying or sharing personal details. Pricing can change — always confirm on the official site before paying.

Children

This directory is a general information website about software tools. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

For any question about this policy, use the contact / “Submit a Tool” email option on our homepage: aitoolsduniya.in. (This site does not publish a separate contact email address; the email options on the homepage open your own email app.)
