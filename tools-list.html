Frequently Asked Questions

What is the AI Tools Directory?

The AI Tools Directory is a curated list of 50 AI tools in 10 categories — Chatbots, Image, Video, Audio & Voice, Writing, Coding, Productivity, Marketing, Design and Research. Each entry gives a short original description, its pricing type and its official website. It is maintained by Rajan Kumar and is available in English and Hindi.

Which AI tool is completely free?

In this directory, Whisper (speech-to-text by OpenAI) is listed as Free. Most other tools are Freemium: they offer a free plan or trial with limits, and paid plans unlock more usage and features.

What is the best AI tool for video?

For AI-generated video clips, popular picks are Runway, Kling AI and Luma Dream Machine. For editing and Shorts, CapCut is the most popular free-to-start option. For turning one long video into many Shorts, OpusClip is built for that job.

What is the best AI tool for voiceover?

ElevenLabs and Murf AI are the popular voiceover tools in this directory. ElevenLabs is known for realistic text-to-speech voices; Murf AI is known for studio-style narration for videos and presentations.

ChatGPT or Claude — which chatbot should I use?

ChatGPT is a general all-rounder for answers, writing, brainstorming and coding help. Claude is known for long-document analysis, careful writing and nuanced reasoning. Both are Freemium, so the practical way to choose is to try the same task on both free plans.

What is the best AI tool for YouTube creators?

A common creator stack from this directory is: ChatGPT or Claude for scripts, ElevenLabs for voiceover, Runway or Kling AI for AI video clips, CapCut for editing, OpusClip for Shorts, and Canva Magic Studio for thumbnails.

Are the tools in this directory free to use?

Each tool in the list is marked Free, Freemium or Paid. Free means no charge for the main use listed; Freemium means a free plan or trial exists with limits; Paid means a subscription is needed. Prices and free limits change, so check the tool's official website before paying.
